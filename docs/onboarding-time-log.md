## How to log an entry

After onboarding a boutique with `scripts/create-boutique.mts` + `scripts/link-tr-boutique-owner.mts` (see `docs/tr-boutique-intake-template.md`), add a row: the date, the boutique slug, total hours from when the client said yes to when the storefront was live and taking real (or sandbox) orders, which single step ate the most time, and anything worth remembering for next time (a field that was confusing, a manual step that should be scripted, etc.).
